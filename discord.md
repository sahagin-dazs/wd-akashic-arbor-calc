## **What is WD Toolbox?**
Free, player-built tools for Wittle Defender: optimize Arbor, track heroes, simulate summons, share lineups/tier lists.  
Main site: **https://wdtoolbox.com**

---

## **🧑‍🚀 Hero Collection**
- Track owned heroes + star levels.
- Filters by role/element/rarity/ownership/search.
- 1‑click **Copy owned heroes** image.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#collection

---

## **🧠 Akashic Arbor Optimizer**
- Set owned heroes, levels, priorities.
- Finds max ATK/HP/DEF% for your team.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#arbor

---

## **🧩 Lineup Builder**
- **PvE**, **PvP**, **GvG**, **Nightmare**, **Guild Boss**.
- Notes + numbered slots + quick add.
- Local saves + shareable view/edit links.
- 1‑click **Copy Image**.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#lineup

---

## **🧭 Skills Explorer**
- Browse every hero skill + base skills.

**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#skills

## **🎲 Summon Simulator**
