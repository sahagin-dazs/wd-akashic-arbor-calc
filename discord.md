## **What is WD Toolbox?**
WD Toolbox is a free set of player‑built tools for Wittle Defenders: optimize your Arbor, track heroes, simulate summons, and share lineups/tier lists. Works best on desktop, great on mobile too.  
Main site: **https://wdtoolbox.com**

---

## **🧑‍🚀 Hero Collection**
- Track owned heroes + star levels.
- Filters by role/element/rarity/ownership/search.
- 1‑click **Copy owned heroes** image (grouped by rarity).
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#collection

---

## **🧠 Akashic Arbor Optimizer**
- Set owned heroes, levels, and priorities.
- Finds max ATK/HP/DEF% for your team.
- Great for comparisons and keeping builds up‑to‑date.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#arbor

---

## **🧩 Lineup Builder**
- **PvE** (5), **PvP** (10), **GvG** (3 teams, 5x2).
- Notes + numbered slots + quick add.
- Local saves + shareable view/edit links.
- 1‑click **Copy Image** for Discord.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#lineup

---

## **🧭 Skills Explorer**
- Browse every hero skill + base skills.
- Filters by element/class/rarity + full-text search.
- 1‑click **Copy Skill Tree** image.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#skills

---

## **🎲 Summon Simulator**
- Simulate **Warrior**, **Rate‑Up**, **Xenoscape** banners.
- Pity/guarantee tracking + history.
- Rate‑Up quest + star‑level tracker.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#summon

---

## **📊 Tier List Builder**
- Custom rows/colors + linked hero combos.
- Reorder heroes + notes.
- Local saves + shareable view/edit links.
- 1‑click **Copy Image**.
**Go here:** https://wdtoolbox.com/#tier

---

## **🔒 Privacy & Safety**
- Local by default; share links are opt‑in.
- Edit links use private tokens.
- Notes are plain text.

---

## **✅ Tips**
- Update **hero star levels** before Arbor sims.
- Use **Configure Existing** in Summon Sim.
- Share **view links** publicly; **edit links** only with trusted people.
